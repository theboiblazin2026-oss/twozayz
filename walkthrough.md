# TWOZAYZ Website - Completed Walkthrough

A custom website built for **TWOZAYZ** (Pressure Washing • Car Detailing • Lawn Care) based directly on the attached flyer design, focused specifically on **Lake City, FL & Columbia County**.

## 🌟 Key Accomplishments

### 1. High-Impact Dark Hydro Design System
- **Official Flyer Banner Branding**: Extracted, isolated, and precision-cleaned the actual graphic banner from the promotional flyer (crown emblem, brush typography "TWOZAYZ", water splashes, and service subtitle) with full alpha transparency and neon glow.
- **Palette**: Dark theme (`#070a12`), electric cyan (`#00d2ff`), vibrant blue (`#0077ff`), lawn green accents (`#10b981`), and metallic borders.
- **Typography**: Google Fonts `Outfit` and `Inter` for crisp readability.

### 2. Complete Content Alignment with Flyer
- **3 Core Service Pillars**:
  - 💦 **Pressure Washing**: Driveways & Sidewalks, House Washing, Decks/Patios & Fences.
  - 🚘 **Car Detailing**: Interior & Exterior, Vacuum & Windows, Tire Shine.
  - 🌿 **Lawn Care**: Precision Mowing, Sharp Edging, Yard Cleanup.
- **Flyer Callouts & Badges**:
  - "FREE ESTIMATES"
  - "Support Local" (Lake City, FL focus)
  - "CALL OR TEXT TODAY"
  - Phone: `(386) 288-1483` & Email: `Twozayz@gmail.com`.

### 3. Interactive Web Features & Lead Routing
- 🧮 **Interactive Free Estimate & Lead Dispatch**:
  - Automatically dispatches notifications directly to `Twozayz@gmail.com` via background API.
  - Generates a 1-touch **"💬 Text Estimate to (386) 288-1483"** pre-filled SMS button right in the confirmation modal so mobile visitors can instantly text their request.
  - Generates a pre-filled **"✉️ Send Email to Twozayz@gmail.com"** fallback action.
- ⭐ **Customer Reviews & Social Proof Section**:
  - 4 verified local customer testimonials covering Pressure Washing, SUV detailing, Lawn Care, and Full house washing in Lake City, FL.
  - 5.0 Star trust badge and verified local homeowner callouts.
- 🔄 **Before & After Visual Comparison Slider**: Interactive tool with pixel-aligned, authentic photography for:
  - 🛻 **Ram 1500 Full Exterior Detail**: Road dirt, mud splatter, and dull panels vs. gleaming mirror gloss silver paint, chrome polish, and dressed tires.
  - ✨ **Chrome Wheel & Tire**: Brake dust caked on chrome spokes and dry faded rubber vs. brilliant mirror shine chrome and deep wet tire dressing.
  - 🔍 **Glass & Water Spot Removal**: Severe mineral etching and clouded towing mirror glass vs. 100% spotless crystal clear reflection.
  - 💦 **Driveway Pressure Washing**: Heavy black algae, tire stains, and Florida mildew vs. spotless bright clean power-washed concrete.
  - 🚘 **Auto Detailing & Tire Shine**: Brake dust coated wheel and faded dry rubber vs. showroom mirror black paint, spotless black alloy rim, and wet tire shine.
  - 🌿 **Lawn Edging & Trim**: Overgrown weeds creeping over sidewalk cracks vs. precision manicured turf with razor-sharp vertical trench edging.
- 🖼️ **Service Card Photo Headers**: Real photo banners embedded on each service card with zoom-on-hover micro-animations.
- 📱 **Mobile First & Sticky Contact Bar**: One-touch Call `(386) 288-1483`, Texting (`sms:386-288-1483`), and Estimate request actions fixed at the bottom of mobile screens.
- 🖼️ **1200x630 OpenGraph & iMessage Share Card**: Custom-crafted social share thumbnail card (`og-preview.jpg`) and Schema.org LocalBusiness structured data for rich previews when texting or sharing the site link.
- 💧 **Live Canvas Particle Engine**: Subtle glowing water splash droplets animating in the background.
